Located in the heart of South America

Bolivia is known for its diverse culture and stunning scenery. Its territory ranges from the Andes Mountains to the Amazon rainforest. Bolivia's food blends indigenous and Spanish traditions, with small touches from neighbouring countries like Peru, Argentina, and Brazil. The foundation of many Bolivian dishes is made up of ingredients like potatoes, corn, quinoa, and cassava. Bolivian cuisine is characterised by spices like chilli and llajwa. Among its most famous drinks are Singani, a Muscat grape spirit, and Paceña, Bolivia's iconic beer. With over 200 traditional dishes, Bolivia offers a vibrant and unique culinary experience for food enthusiasts.

Fun fact about Bolivia!

Bolivia has over 4,000 different types of potatoes, and they are a vital part of Bolivian gastronomy. Almost every traditional dish includes potatoes in some form, and there are even dishes that feature both fresh and freeze-dried potatoes together!
