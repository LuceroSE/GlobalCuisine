The Republic of Lithuania

Lithuania is a hidden gem among the crowd of countries; with beautiful landscapes and traditions awaiting to be discovered! It's located off the coast of the Baltic Sea, to the top right of Poland, below Latvia and Estonia. Lithuanian food gives you a true taste of Eastern-European flavours; it's something that you truly have to experience for yourselves. Lithuanian dishes range anywhere from being meat-based, to being completely vegetarian. Care for a hot dish? We have cepelinai. Would you like something cold instead? We have Cold Beet Soup. Something sweet instead? We have honey cake. Cake too sweet? Try Žagarėliai instead. And luckily for those who cannot stand spice, most dishes don't generally contain any spicy ingredients. Lithuanian cuisine takes your average ingredients, and turns them into mouthwatering experiences

Lithuanian Fact!

As weird as it may sound, Lithuania has its own unique scent, dubbed "The Scent of Lithuania". If you ever plan on eating traditional Lithuanian dishes, why not go all out and get the whole experience? Lithuania's landscape, traditions and scent will surely leave you with a strong memory!

This page contains a common Lithuanian starter, two well known main dishes and a delectable dessert. Explore from our range to let your taste buds run wild and create a memorable experience!
