About Bangladesh

Bangladesh is a country located in South Asia, it's a vibrant and densely populated country known for its rich cultural landscapes, lush green landscapes, and the majestic rivers that cut through its terrain. The country's capital Dhaka is a bustling city, while the Sundarbans, a UNESCO World Heritage site, is famous for the royal Bengal Tiger and its diverse ecosystem.

Bangladesh is renowned for its flavorful and diverse cuisine, deeply rooted in its agricultural heritage. Rice, lentils, and fish form the foundation of Bangladeshi meals, complemented by a variety of spices like turmeric, mustard seeds, and chili, which create rich and aromatic dishes. Farming is central to life in Bangladesh, with its fertile delta supporting the cultivation of rice and vegetables, which greatly influences the cuisine and adds unique textures and flavors to the dishes. The geography of Bangladesh also influences its cuisine greatly as there are are over 700 rivers in the country as a result fish is a central part of the culture in Bangladesh.
