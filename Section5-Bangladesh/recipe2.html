Shorshe Ilish (Hilsa Fish in Mustard Sauce)

About the Dish

Shorshe Ilish is one of the most iconic dishes in Bangladeshi cuisine, featuring the delicate Hilsa fish cooked in a rich mustard sauce. It's a perfect blend of tangy mustard, bold spices, and the tender texture of Ilish fish.

Ingredients

  • 4 pieces Hilsa Fish (Ilish)
  • 2 tbsp Mustard Paste
  • 4-5 Green Chilies
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 3 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1/2 tsp Salt

Instructions

  1. Wash the Hilsa fish pieces thoroughly and pat them dry.
  2. Marinate the fish with salt and turmeric powder. Set aside for 10 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix mustard paste with a little water to form a smooth consistency.
  4. Heat mustard oil in a pan until it starts smoking. Reduce heat.
  5. Add the mustard paste and green chilies to the oil and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  6. Place the fish pieces gently in the sauce and simmer for 8-10 minutes, turning once.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice.
