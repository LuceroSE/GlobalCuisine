About the Dish

Chhanar Payesh is a traditional Bangladeshi dessert made with fresh chhena (paneer) and simmered in milk, sugar, and aromatic spices like cardamom. This sweet treat is perfect for celebrations and festivals, offering a melt-in-your-mouth experience!

Ingredients

  • 1 liter Full Cream Milk
  • 1 cup Fresh Chhena (paneer)
  • 1/2 cup Sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Cardamom Powder
  • 2 tablespoons Chopped Almonds and Pistachios (for garnish)
  • A few strands of Saffron (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring the milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Reduce the heat and simmer until it reduces to about half its original volume.
  2. Add sugar and cardamom powder to the milk. Stir well to dissolve the sugar.
  3. Gently add the fresh chhena (paneer) into the milk. Stir carefully to avoid breaking the chhena.
  4. Simmer for another 10-15 minutes until the milk thickens and coats the chhena pieces.
  5. If using saffron, soak the strands in a tablespoon of warm milk and add it to the mixture for flavor and color.
  6. Turn off the heat and let the Chhanar Payesh cool. Garnish with chopped almonds and pistachios.
  7. Refrigerate for a few hours before serving chilled.
