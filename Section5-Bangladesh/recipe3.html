Biryani (Fragrant Spiced Rice with Meat)

About the Dish

Biryani is a beloved dish across Bangladesh, known for its aromatic flavors and tender meat. This dish combines fragrant spiced rice, saffron, and slow-cooked meat to create a hearty, flavorful meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 500g Chicken or Mutton
  • 2 Onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1 cup Yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1/4 cup Mustard Oil
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chili Powder
  • 2-3 Cardamom Pods
  • 2-3 Cloves
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup Fried Onions (optional, for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice in water for 30 minutes. Drain before cooking.
  2. Marinate the chicken/mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt. Set aside for 1 hour.
  3. In a pot, heat mustard oil and fry sliced onions until golden brown. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add the marinated meat to the pot and cook on medium heat until tender.
  5. In a separate pot, boil water with cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and salt. Add rice and cook until 70% done. Drain.
  6. Layer the meat and rice in a pot, starting and ending with rice. Add fried onions between layers.
  7. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with a side of raita or salad.
