About the Dish

Mishti Doi, or sweetened yogurt, is a traditional dessert enjoyed throughout Bangladesh. Made with yogurt, sugar, and sometimes flavored with cardamom, this dish is popular for its rich, creamy texture and subtly sweet flavor. It’s a perfect end to a meal, often served chilled for a refreshing finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 liter Full Cream Milk
  • 200g Sugar
  • 1/2 cup Yogurt (for culture)
  • 1/4 tsp Cardamom Powder (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the milk in a heavy-bottomed pan until it reduces to about 3/4 of its original quantity.
  2. Add the sugar to the milk and stir until it dissolves completely.
  3. Allow the milk to cool down to lukewarm (about 35-40°C). Stir in the yogurt culture and cardamom powder (if using).
  4. Pour the milk mixture into a clean container or clay pot, and cover it to keep it warm.
  5. Let the yogurt set for 6-8 hours or overnight in a warm place. Once set, refrigerate for a few hours before serving.
  6. Serve chilled, garnished with some cardamom or a few strands of saffron (optional).
