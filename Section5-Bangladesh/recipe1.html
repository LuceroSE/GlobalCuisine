Shutki Bharta (Fermented Dried Fish Mash)

About the Dish

Shutki Bharta is a traditional Bangladeshi starter known for its bold, umami-packed flavors. Prepared with dried fermented fish, onions, mustard oil, and chilies, this dish is a rustic favorite that pairs wonderfully with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 100g Fermented Dried Fish (Shutki)
  • 2 tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 2 Onions (finely chopped)
  • 3 Green Chilies (chopped)
  • 1 Garlic Clove (crushed)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Wash the dried fish thoroughly under running water to remove excess salt and impurities.
  2. Lightly roast the fish in a pan without oil until it becomes fragrant and crispy.
  3. In a separate pan, heat mustard oil and sauté the onions, garlic, and green chilies until softened.
  4. Add the roasted fish to the pan and mash everything together using a wooden spoon.
  5. Season with salt, if needed, and mash until smooth and well-combined.
  6. Serve as a starter with steamed rice.
